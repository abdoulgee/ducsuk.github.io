MISSION STATEMENT

Sovereignty, Safety, &
The Right To Dwell

DUCSUK CIC is a community-led grassroots organization dedicated to the peaceful activation of derelict urban spaces for the benefit of Dundee’s most vulnerable citizens.

We believe that land neglect is a civic failure, and we stand at the intersection of Human Rights advocacy and Advanced Community Safety.

Legal & Moral Foundation

Operating under the European Convention on Human Rights:

  • Article 8: We defend the fundamental right of every individual to have a place of residence and dignity, standing against the systemic displacement of the homeless.
  • Article 11: We exercise our right to peacefully gather and organize for social, cultural, and political improvement.

Unrivaled Security & Oversight

Unlike traditional grassroots movements, DUCSUK is managed with the precision of a professional security firm.

01

The Birmingham Standard

Protocols derived from elite UK standards, including Counter-Terrorism trained personnel and a core team of 6 SIA-licensed professionals.

02

Elite Management

Site Safety overseen by a team formerly attached to multi-national organizations, ensuring the most secure, fire-safe, and disciplined sites in Scotland.

03

Peaceful Deterrence

Our presence is a "Safety Shield" that allows creative expression and crisis support to flourish without interference.
